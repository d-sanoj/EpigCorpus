F7. M1 with and without the withheld provinces in training. On provinces seen in
training the province conditioning is worth 8.3 points. On Britannia, Mauretania
Caesariensis and Pannonia inferior, withheld entirely, it is worth exactly zero to four
decimal places: the conditional lookup table is empty for an unseen province, so C2 and
C3 back off to C1. The gain is memorisation of local distributions, not a transferable
property of Latin.
